# A4 notes: reconstruction ladder

Code: `shared/reconstruct.js` (ladder, policies), `shared/reconstruct-match.js` (one-to-one track
assignment), `desktop/reconstruct-store.js` (attempts, provider cache, ledger commits),
`desktop/jobs/reconstruct-job.js` (job class), `desktop/musicbrainz.js` (client).

## Routes

Stages run in cost order: decisions, local tags, folder structure, provider cache, MusicBrainz IDs,
MusicBrainz search, AcoustID. An album stops at the first route that supports it. Each route records
one outcome: succeeded, no-match, ambiguous, not-applicable, unavailable, deferred,
retryable-failure or not-permitted-by-user. A failed or over-budget lookup is retryable or deferred,
never a negative answer. AcoustID needs its own consent and key; with consent but no key it is
`unavailable`. Every change commits through the ledger, authored by its route, on deterministic
evidence, so it can be undone. Locks and user values are never touched.

## Consent

With online lookups off, the local routes still run (field repairs, local albums) and every online
route says `not-permitted-by-user`, naming `online-lookups` in `blockedBy`. Granting consent makes
those albums appear in `reconstructionQueue` again. The offline test installs a network guard
(`desktop/tests/network-guard.js`) and fails on any outbound request.

## Licences

MusicBrainz core data is stored as CC0-1.0 observations. Tags, genres and ratings are dropped when a
release is reduced and are never cached or stored (asserted on observations and the cache). The
client paces requests at 1100 ms, spends a request budget per album and sends a generic User-Agent.

## Answer-key corpus

`desktop/tests/reconstruct-corpus.js` damages copies of the test library (missing album tag,
inconsistent artist, split discs, compilation, partial album tag, mis-numbered tracks, loose files).
MusicBrainz answers are recorded in `desktop/tests/fixtures/musicbrainz-corpus.json`; re-record with
`KEPT_RECORD_MUSICBRAINZ=1` (real requests, 1 per second).

| Run | False merges | Split groups repaired | Merge precision | Albums resolved |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| offline | 0 | 4 of 4 | 100% | 25 of 34 |
| recorded lookups | 0 | 4 of 4 | 100% | 26 of 34 |

Offline, editions stay unknown. With recorded answers the album with no tag is named by its tracklist
(the title the key expects).

## Dry run on a real folder

`node desktop/scripts/reconstruct-dry-run.mjs <folder> [--live]` scans read-only into a temporary
catalog; nothing is written to the folder or to a profile. `--live` grants lookups for at most 10
albums at 1 request per second.

On a private real collection, read-only (958 files, 55 albums): offline, 1 album repaired by local tags and 54 had
nothing to repair; 52 resolved and 3 unresolved. With lookups on for 10 albums: 1 identified by
MusicBrainz search, 5 no match, 4 not applicable; the album count stayed 55 (no merges).

## Known gaps

AcoustID fingerprinting has no fingerprinter yet, so the route is `unavailable` even with consent and
a key. The job's real-time pause for playback is covered by A2, not re-measured here.
