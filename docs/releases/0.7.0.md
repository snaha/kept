## What's new

- **Music is now Kept.** App names, installers and launchers use the new name. Existing libraries and profiles are retained.
- **Control playback from your phone.** Share on your trusted home network or through Tailscale. Album Play responds to the first tap in mobile Safari, even when the toolbar is hidden.
- **More ways to browse.** Cover Flow, optional glossy sleeves, improved album actions, and a shortcut to reveal the playing album and return to your previous view.
- **A smoother library.** Persistent rescan progress, covers loaded nearest the viewport first, artist sorting by default, and filters for albums without artwork.
- **Safer sharing and more resilient playback.** Sharing accounts stay non-admin, credentials are scoped to their server and account, and malformed requests are contained.

## Upgrading

Quit Music or Kept before opening this release. Kept retains the existing Music data directory; Kept Preview keeps its separate preview directory. After confirming the new app works, you can remove the old Music application bundle. Keep your music folders and app data.

For portable downloads, keep the Data folder and use the included Open Kept launcher. Audio files remain in the folders you choose.

Mac downloads support Apple Silicon and are **not notarized**. Follow the Done → Open Anyway instructions below on first launch. Home-network HTTP sharing remains unencrypted; use it only on a trusted network or through Tailscale.
