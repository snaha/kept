KEPT — MAC INSTALLATION

This build has not been notarized by Apple. macOS will display
"Apple could not verify" on first launch. Open Anyway is required.

1. Drag the app to Applications.
2. Open the app from Applications. In the "Apple could not verify"
   warning, click Done, NOT Move to Trash. Done keeps the app installed.
3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security and scroll to Security.
4. Choose Open Anyway for this app. Confirm Open Anyway if asked,
   then approve with Touch ID or your Mac password.
5. Open the app again if it does not launch automatically.

The same steps apply to the ZIP download.
These instructions apply to "Apple could not verify", not a warning
that malware was detected.

Only approve a build you trust. Do not disable Gatekeeper globally.
Apple's instructions: https://support.apple.com/en-us/102445

Kept Preview uses separate data from the regular Kept app.
Existing Music data (or Music Preview data) is retained after the rename.
