KEPT PREVIEW — NOT NOTARIZED

This preview has not been notarized by Apple. macOS will display
"Apple could not verify" on first launch. Open Anyway is required.

1. Drag Kept Preview to Applications.
2. Open Kept Preview from Applications, then dismiss the warning.
3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security.
4. Choose Open Anyway for Kept Preview and confirm.

Only approve a build you trust. Do not disable Gatekeeper globally.
Apple's instructions: https://support.apple.com/en-us/102445

The preview uses separate data from the regular Kept app.
Existing Music Preview data is retained after the rename.
